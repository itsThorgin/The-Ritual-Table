# The Ritual Table

Bring a question, choose cards or runes, and give your attention somewhere to land. The Ritual Table is a small browser app for fun, possible self-discovery and reflection on the side. Cosmic certainty is not included.

Prepare your reading, reveal it one position at a time, and explore the meanings and questions. Keep what makes sense to you. You are still the person making decisions.

## What it is based on

The Tarot branch uses the 78 cards, upright and reversed meanings, and spread rules from the **The Gothic Tarot** handbook by Joseph Vargo. Additional position questions and connections are original app content, shown separately from the handbook meanings. Card faces use simple local frames and symbols.

The Rune branch uses the **24 Elder Futhark runes**, with no blank rune and no reversals. Historical names and imagery are checked against the National Museum of Denmark, scholarly comparisons by Scott T. Shell and Frederick George Jones, Jr., and the later Old English, Norwegian and Icelandic rune poems translated by Bruce Dickins. Modern layout research also included Diana L. Paxson's *Taking Up the Runes*.

The Rune questions and reflections are original. The layouts are modern conventions or adaptations, with inspiration from Norn imagery, the World Tree and *Sigrdrífumál*. Later rune poems help explain the background; they are not a surviving Elder Futhark instruction manual. Historical uncertainty is kept visible in each rune's details.

## Choose your reading

### Tarot

- **The Mystic Seven:** seven cards for a focused question. Explore the past, present, future, advised path, influences, obstacles and outcome.
- **The Celtic Cross:** ten dealt cards plus a chosen upright signifier representing you or your situation. A broader view of influences, circumstances, hopes, concerns and possible direction.

Reversals are optional. You can also cut the deck, apply optional color or number choices as personal touches to the randomness, or follow the six-round Celtic preparation guide. Only preparation you actually apply after initial shuffling of the deck affects the deck.

### Runes

- **Daily Counsel, 1 rune:** one thing to notice, to think about today.
- **Three Norns, 3 runes:** choose three concealed lots before beginning. Read them as Roots, Present and Responsibility: what shaped this, what is unfolding, and what response deserves consideration.
- **Elemental Reflection, 5 runes:** explore the situation through practical foundations, understanding, motivation and feeling.
- **Seven Counsels, 7 runes:** questions about resolve, discernment, care, journey, repair, speech and wisdom. An original adaptation inspired by *Sigrdrífumál*.
- **Nine Worlds Reflection, 9 runes:** a modern World Tree layout for daily life, stillness, drive, nourishment, challenge, perspective, inner work, values and inheritance.

Each mode supports revisiting revealed symbols. Complete readings also offer optional connections and a recap.

## What happens under the cloth

The app uses your browser's cryptographic random generator to shuffle without replacement. Beginning a reading fixes the full prepared deck or rune bag and every assignment. Revealing, inspecting and downloading cannot quietly change the result. The symbols get one job each.

Readings stay in memory. Closing or refreshing clears them. Once every position is revealed, you can explicitly download a TXT archive containing the reading and its preparation record. That file keeps the words, but cannot restore a session.

## Privacy and security

The app itself doesn't collect or send any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, tracking cookies or browser storage, and requests no camera or microphone access.

All reading logic, meanings, symbols and styles are bundled with the app. No remote libraries, fonts, APIs or internet lookups are needed during a reading. A restrictive Content Security Policy blocks app connections and external resources. The universe does not need your email address, and neither do I.

When hosted online, the browser downloads the app files from that host.

## Open the table

Use a modern browser with JavaScript and cryptographic randomness available. Choose Tarot or Runes, prepare, shuffle -> stop shuffling and begin. There is nothing to install, compile or subscribe to.
